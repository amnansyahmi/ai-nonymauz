Each system works a little differently.
Here are the essentials.

Are all the systems connected?

They are part of the same product family, but each has its own workspace and setup. A single shared account or automatic data sync is not currently offered.

Which system should I start with?

Choose People for HR, KretivOS for a creative business workspace, PowerOS for business operations, FlowTrack for personal finances, or Restu.ai for wedding planning. The AI and development tools are listed separately.

Can I use them on my phone?

The product family includes responsive web apps, and several support adding the app to your home screen. Installation and offline behaviour depend on the individual system.

Are AI features available in every system?

AI capabilities vary by system and may require configuration. Open a system’s details to see its focus; check the app itself for current availability and setup requirements.

Can a system be adapted for my business?

Start by writing down your workflow, users, and the problem you want to solve. The project brief below helps you organise those details before discussing a custom system.
